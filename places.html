Top 10 Must-Visit Places in Bihar

From UNESCO World Heritage monasteries and Mauryan rock-cut caves to pristine Himalayan tiger reserves.

#01 • UNESCO Heritage
📍 Bodh Gaya, Gaya District

Mahabodhi Temple Complex

One of the four holy sites related to the life of Lord Buddha, and the exact spot where he attained supreme enlightenment beneath the sacred Bodhi Tree. Originally built by Emperor Ashoka in the 3rd century BCE, the 55-meter pyramidal brick temple stands as a global beacon of peace and meditation.

Best TimeOct – Mar
Nearest AirportGaya International (12 km)
HighlightSacred Bodhi Tree & Vajrasana
#02 • UNESCO Heritage
📍 Nalanda District

Ancient Nalanda Mahavihara

Founded in the 5th century CE during the Gupta Empire, Nalanda was the world's first great residential international university. For over 800 years, more than 10,000 students and 2,000 scholars from China, Korea, Tibet, and Central Asia studied astronomy, medicine, logic, and Buddhist philosophy here.

Best TimeOct – Mar
Nearest StationRajgir / Nalanda
HighlightSariputta Stupa & Museum
#03 • Spiritual & Scenic
📍 Rajgir, Nalanda District

Rajgir & Vishwa Shanti Stupa

The ancient capital of the Magadha Empire encircled by seven picturesque hills. Revered by both Buddhists and Jains, Rajgir features the gleaming white Vishwa Shanti Stupa atop Ratnagiri Hill (accessible via aerial ropeway), Gridhakuta (Vulture's Peak), and therapeutic Brahmakund hot springs.

Best TimeOct – Mar
AttractionRopeway & Glass Skywalk
Significance1st Buddhist Council Site
#04 • Sacred Takht
📍 Patna City, Patna

Takht Sri Harmandir Ji Patna Sahib

Revered as one of the five Takhts (seats of authority) of Sikhism, this magnificent white-marble gurudwara commemorates the birthplace of the tenth Sikh Guru, Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji (born 1666). Built by Maharaja Ranjit Singh, it houses sacred relics, ancient weapons, and handwritten hukamnamas.

Best TimeYear-Round (Prakash Parv)
ConnectivityPatna Sahib Jn. (2 km)
HighlightGuru Ka Langar & Museum
#05 • Architectural Gem
📍 Sasaram, Rohtas District

Tomb of Sher Shah Suri

Often hailed as the "Second Taj Mahal of India," this towering 122-foot octagonal mausoleum built in 1545 stands on a stone plinth in the middle of a vast square lake. Crafted from red Chunar sandstone, it represents the pinnacle of Afghan Indo-Islamic architecture in India.

Best TimeOct – Mar
Built In1545 CE
HighlightLake Causeway & Grand Dome
#06 • Ancient Seat
📍 Antichak, Bhagalpur District

Vikramshila Mahavihara

Established by King Dharmapala of the Pala dynasty in the late 8th century CE, Vikramshila rivaled Nalanda as a premier center of Buddhist scholarship. Its massive cruciform central stupa surrounded by 208 monastic cells and terracotta plaques reflects remarkable monastic engineering.

Best TimeNov – Feb
Distance40 km from Bhagalpur
HighlightCruciform Stupa & Terracotta Art
#07 • Tallest Stupa
📍 Kesaria, East Champaran

Kesaria Buddha Stupa

Rising to a staggering height of 104 feet with a circumference of nearly 1,400 feet, Kesaria Stupa is recognized as the tallest and largest excavated Buddhist stupa in the world. It marks the spot where Lord Buddha gifted his alms bowl to the Licchavis of Vaishali before attaining Mahaparinirvana.

Best TimeOct – Mar
Height104 Feet (31.7 m)
HighlightMulti-Tiered Brick Terraces
#08 • Jain Tirtha
📍 Pawapuri, Nalanda District

Jal Mandir, Pawapuri

Set in the center of a tranquil tank filled with blooming pink and white lotuses, Jal Mandir is one of the holiest pilgrimage destinations for Jains worldwide. It marks the sacred cremation site of Lord Mahavira, the 24th Tirthankara, who attained Moksha here around 527 BCE.

Best TimeOct – Mar (Diwali Festival)
ArchitectureWhite Marble Vimana
Highlight600-ft Stone Bridge & Lotus Lake
#09 • Mauryan Wonder
📍 Makhdumpur, Jehanabad District

Barabar Rock-Cut Caves

Carved out of monolithic granite hills during the reign of Emperor Ashoka (3rd century BCE), Barabar Caves are India's oldest surviving rock-cut caves. The Lomas Rishi Cave features an iconic chaitya arch facade, while the interiors boast an astonishing mirror-like polish and uncanny acoustic echo.

Best TimeOct – Mar
Era3rd Century BCE (Mauryan)
HighlightLomas Rishi Arch & Echo Halls
#10 • Wildlife Reserve
📍 Valmikinagar, West Champaran

Valmiki Tiger Reserve & National Park

Located at the foothills of the Himalayas along the Indo-Nepal border and the Gandak River, Valmiki National Park is Bihar's only Tiger Reserve. Spanning over 890 sq. km of lush sal forests and grasslands, it shelters Royal Bengal Tigers, Indian rhinoceroses, leopards, and over 250 bird species.

Best TimeNov – Apr
ActivitiesJungle Safari & River Rafting
HighlightRoyal Bengal Tigers & Terai Mist
