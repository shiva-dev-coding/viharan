Heartland of Eastern India

Where Empires & Wisdom Were Born

Nestled along the fertile plains of the sacred Ganga River, Bihar is the land where Gautama Buddha attained enlightenment, Lord Mahavira preached non-violence, and Guru Gobind Singh Ji was born.

From the Mauryan and Gupta golden ages to the world's earliest residential universities at Nalanda and Vikramshila, every corner of Bihar tells a story that shaped human civilization.
