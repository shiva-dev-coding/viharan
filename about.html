About Viharan & Bihar

Derived from the Sanskrit word "Vihara" (meaning sacred monastery and serene wandering), Viharan celebrates Bihar's eternal legacy.

The Land of Enlightenment

Why Bihar Matters to the World

Few regions on Earth rival Bihar's contribution to human philosophy, governance, and science. From the ancient Republic of Vaishali—the world's earliest organized democracy—to Emperor Ashoka's edicts of peace, Bihar laid the moral foundations of Indian civilization.

Mathematician and astronomer Aryabhata pioneered the concept of zero and planetary motion in ancient Kusumapura (Patna), while global seekers walked the cloisters of Nalanda and Vikramshila.

🏛 Our Mission

To showcase Bihar's authentic historical monuments, sacred pilgrimage circuits, GI-tagged arts, and culinary heritage in a modern, accessible digital experience for travelers worldwide.

🕉 Multi-Faith Harmony

Bihar is a unique spiritual confluence where Hinduism, Buddhism, Jainism, Sikhism, and Sufism share centuries of peaceful coexistence and sacred landmarks.

🌾 Living Traditions

Beyond ancient ruins, Bihar thrives through its warm hospitality, lyrical languages (Maithili, Bhojpuri, Magahi, Angika, Bajjika), and vibrant rural artistry.

Bihar at a Glance

Capital City Patna (Ancient Pataliputra)
State Foundation Day 22 March (Bihar Diwas)
Major Languages Hindi, Maithili, Bhojpuri, Magahi, Angika, Bajjika, Urdu
UNESCO World Heritage Sites Mahabodhi Temple Complex (Bodh Gaya) & Nalanda Mahavihara
State Symbols Tree: Peepal (Bodhi Tree) • Flower: Kachnar • Bird: House Sparrow • Animal: Gaur
Prominent GI-Tagged Products Madhubani Painting, Silao Khaja, Mithila Makhana, Bhagalpuri Silk, Shahi Litchi, Katarni Rice, Jardalu Mango
