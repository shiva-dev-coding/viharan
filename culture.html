Living Culture, Art & Festivals

Experience the timeless devotion of Chhath Puja, GI-tagged Mithila paintings, intricate handicrafts, and historic fairs.

Mahaparva Festival

Chhath Puja

Bihar's grandest eco-friendly Vedic festival dedicated to Surya Dev (the Sun God) and Chhathi Maiya. Millions gather along riverbanks at sunset and sunrise to offer Arghya with bamboo soops, sugarcane, and Thekua without priests or idol worship.

Celebrated: Kartika Shukla Shashthi (Oct/Nov)
GI-Tagged Folk Art

Madhubani (Mithila) Painting

Dating back to the Ramayana era, Mithila painting uses natural plant-derived pigments, twigs, and nib pens to create geometric patterns, mythological epics, and nature motifs with zero empty space left on the canvas.

Region: Madhubani & Darbhanga, Mithila
Sacred Wedding Mural

Kohbar Art

A deeply symbolic branch of Mithila art painted on the walls of the bridal chamber (Kohbar Ghar). Featuring lotus leaves, bamboo groves, fish, and tortoises, Kohbar symbolizes fertility, prosperity, and marital harmony.

Tradition: Maithil Matrimonial Heritage
Angika Scroll Art

Manjusha Art

Originating in the Anga region (Bhagalpur), Manjusha is a sequential narrative art form painted exclusively in pink, green, and yellow—depicting the timeless folklore of Bihula and Bala Lakhindar on temple-shaped ceremonial boxes.

Region: Bhagalpur (Anga Region)
GI-Tagged Textile

Sujini Embroidery

An ancient rural quilting tradition where women stitch layers of soft recycled cloth together using fine running stitches to narrate stories of village life, nature, and social empowerment.

Region: Bhusura, Muzaffarpur
Eco-Handicraft

Sikki Grass Craft

Artisans in Mithila weave golden-hued wild Sikki river grass into vibrant baskets (pauti), figurines, and ceremonial boxes dyed with natural colors—an essential part of traditional bridal trousseaus.

Status: GI-Tagged Sustainable Craft
Royal Craftsmanship

Muzaffarpur Lac Bangle Craft

Handcrafted from natural tree resin (lah) and studded with sparkling stones and mirrors, Muzaffarpur's bridal lac bangles are revered across India as auspicious symbols of marriage and festivity.

Hub: Islampur, Muzaffarpur
GI-Tagged Supercrop

Mithila Makhana Heritage

Bihar produces over 85% of the world's fox nuts (Makhana). Skilled Mallah community divers harvest prickly water-lily seeds from Mithila's wetlands and roast-pop them using ancestral techniques.

Region: Darbhanga, Madhubani & Purnia
Asia's Largest Cattle Fair

Sonepur Mela (Harihar Kshetra)

Held every Kartik Purnima at the sacred confluence of the Ganga and Gandak rivers, this centuries-old fair dates back to Chandragupta Maurya's era, blending holy river baths, folk theater, and vibrant rural trade.

Location: Sonepur, Saran District
Sacred Triennial Fair

Malmas Mela, Rajgir

Observed once every three years during the lunar leap month (Adhik Maas), millions of devotees gather in Rajgir to bathe in the sacred Brahmakund hot springs amidst vibrant illuminated fairs and cultural performances.

Location: Rajgir, Nalanda District
